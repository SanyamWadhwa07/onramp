# Code of conduct

Onramp adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, give and accept constructive criticism
gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people who use and build this project. Harassment,
insults, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not accepted.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you
represent the project) and to everyone who takes part.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@SanyamWadhwa07](https://github.com/SanyamWadhwa07), using the contact details on their GitHub
profile. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments,
commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors from the project
temporarily or permanently, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
